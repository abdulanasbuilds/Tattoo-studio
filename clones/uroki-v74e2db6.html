About

Uroki Tattoo Studio is located in the heart of Warsaw's Powiśle, on Aleja 3 Maja, inside a beautifully restored historic tenement house just steps from the Poniatowski Bridge. Built on passion, professionalism, and a welcoming atmosphere, our studio is a place where creativity thrives—and where our clients always feel at home.

Uroki Tattoo Studio was founded by Marcin Łukasiewicz, a tattoo artist with over 15 years of experience. His dedication to exceptional craftsmanship and his careful selection of talented artists have shaped a studio built on creativity, precision, and trust.

**Uroki Tattoo Studio** is more than a place to get tattooed—it's a creative space where art lives beyond the skin. Alongside exceptional tattoo craftsmanship, we showcase original paintings, illustrations, and graphic works that inspire our artists and clients alike. Every piece reflects our passion for visual storytelling, creating an atmosphere where creativity, expression, and timeless artistry come together.

Our gallery is designed to spark imagination, inspire creativity, and immerse you in the world of contemporary tattoo art. More than a display of artwork, it's a reflection of our passion and vision. Every piece reinforces what we stand for—a studio of skilled professionals dedicated to exceptional craftsmanship, creativity, and an experience you can trust.
