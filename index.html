/ 08 — Good to know
Before you start

Questions,
answered.

01

How do I book?

+

Send a note through the booking form with your idea, placement, approximate size, and reference images. We reply within 48 hours.

02

Do you take walk-ins?

+

We are appointment-led. A limited flash window opens every Saturday when noted in our booking calendar.

03

Can I bring my own design?

+

Yes. We love working from your references, then refining scale, placement, and line quality with you.

04

What about aftercare?

+

Every session includes a printed aftercare guide and a follow-up check-in tailored to your piece.
