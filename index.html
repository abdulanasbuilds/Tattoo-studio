BEFORE YOU START

Clear answers
for a calm
appointment.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome when the schedule allows. Custom work is best started through the booking form.

How much does a tattoo cost?

Quotes depend on size, placement, detail and time. Send the brief first for an accurate conversation.

How should I prepare?

Arrive hydrated, fed and rested. Wear clothing that gives easy access to the placement and avoid alcohol before your session.

What happens after the session?

You leave with clear aftercare instructions and a direct line for questions during the healing process.
