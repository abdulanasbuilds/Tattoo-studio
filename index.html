FAQ · 明確に答える

WHAT YOU
NEED TO KNOW
BEFORE WE START.

How do we start working together?

We begin with a short call to understand scope, timeline and priorities. If there’s a fit, we define the engagement and start with a clear plan.

Who will I work with day to day?

The same person who leads the work stays involved throughout. No handoffs, no layered communication.

What’s included in the monthly hours?

Design, direction and iteration within the agreed scope. That includes brand, web and product work depending on the plan.

What happens if the scope changes?

We adjust the scope together before continuing. No silent overages, no unclear extensions.

Can I cancel or pause the engagement?

Yes. Engagements run month to month with a 30-day notice.

What do you need from me to get started?

Clarity on goals, access to existing materials and a single point of contact.
