ABOUT SEIDU

A multidisciplinary
visual artist.

Based in East Legon, Seidu works between permanent body art, physical painting and meticulous pencil illustration.

Seidu is one of the young professional tattoo artists building a distinct visual language in Accra, with a practice that moves naturally from skin to canvas to paper.

His work is rooted in precision and patience: clean custom tattoos, physical paintings and realistic pencil pieces. That range is part of the identity, not an extra.

For clients, the goal is simple — take a rough idea, a reference or a feeling and turn it into something considered and personal.

“Precision, patience, and passion.”
